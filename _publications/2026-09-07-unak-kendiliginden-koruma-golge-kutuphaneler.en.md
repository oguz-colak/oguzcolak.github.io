This paper will be presented at the ÜNAK '26 Symposium on 7–8 September 2026. The study conceptually addresses the self-preservation function of shadow libraries in terms of sustainability and accessibility of digital content.

### Abstract
In the digital preservation literature, the position and functions of self-preservation mechanisms created by shadow libraries within the ecosystem are discussed alongside formal infrastructures.

### Key Topics
*   Shadow libraries and digital preservation.
*   Self-preservation infrastructures.
*   Strategic position of information centers.
