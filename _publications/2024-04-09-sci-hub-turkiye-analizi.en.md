This study examines the representation of Turkish-affiliated scientific publications on the Sci-Hub platform and the dynamics of access to these publications.

### Current Status
The project is currently **in progress**. Data collection and analysis phases are ongoing.

### Project Summary
The relationship between access channels for researchers in Turkey and Sci-Hub usage is analyzed within the framework of the open science ecosystem. The study will discuss the bibliometric significance of such alternative access channels, particularly for institutions with limited budgets.
