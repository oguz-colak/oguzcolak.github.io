This study is a master's thesis completed at the Graduate School of Social Sciences, Çankırı Karatekin University.

### Abstract
The thesis uses various bibliometric methods to measure the international visibility and impact of local scientific journals. The study evaluates journal performance by analyzing the balance between locality and universality in academic publishing.
